# A-13. Levels, persistence, alert events

**Phase:** A | **Depends on:** A-12 | **Read:** `docs/CONTRACTS.md` and `history/plan/components/05-detection.md sections 3-4`

Load **only** those two documents plus this file. Do not read the rest of `history/plan/`.

---

## Build

Level from the **residual**, not the deviation. Alert event as a run of consecutive nights at
YELLOW or above containing at least one RED. An unusable night breaks the run.

---

## Tests

Write these first, then make them pass.

- `test_level_thresholds_exact` -- 2.99, 3.0, 3.99, 4.0
- `test_level_uses_residual_not_deviation`
- `test_single_red_night_does_not_flag`
- `test_two_consecutive_with_one_red_flags`
- `test_two_yellows_without_red_does_not_flag`
- `test_unusable_night_breaks_the_run`
- `test_flag_fires_on_the_night_the_run_completes`
- `test_detector_never_reads_future_nights`
- `test_negative_residual_is_green_not_clamped`
- `test_cold_rung_still_computes_detection`

## Do not

- The detector must never read a night after the one it is computing.
- Do not clamp a negative residual to zero.
