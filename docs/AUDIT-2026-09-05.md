WHAT HOLDS. causes.yaml (12 rows) and probes.yaml (8 rows) carry a per-row `provenance`, it is validated at load (src/wha/belief/causes.py:88, src/wha/policy/probes.py:158) and pinned by tests/unit/test_knowledge_provenance.py. The ASSUMED marking genuinely reaches the screen on a real production path: src/wha/ui/history.py:184 -> render_episode_panel -> render_summary_tab -> api().get_knowledge_provenance() (HTTP GET /knowledge/provenance, src/wha/api/app.py:390 -> src/wha/service/belief.py:114, which reads the YAML) -> an "Evidence: not measured" column plus a named warning (src/wha/ui/components/episode_panel.py:71-95). Probe provenance is also emitted inline in each reason (src/wha/agent/differential.py:70) and into the corpus passage (src/wha/retrieve/knowledge_docs.py:530). The "Swept." annotations are genuinely gone from causes.yaml and probes.yaml. The infection and band-width measurements are reproducible: tools/cohort/infection.py and tools/cohort/band_width.py exist, are 300 and 193 lines of real code, and data/cohort/COVID-19-Wearables.zip is present.

WHAT FAILS. red_flags.yaml and triage_rules.yaml contain ZERO `provenance` fields (grep -c "provenance:" returns 0 for both). Eleven safety numbers in red_flags.yaml -- the two robust-z bounds, the two cold-rung absolute bpm bounds, six persistence windows and the negation window -- carry no provenance, no citation, no ASSUMED label, appear in no docs/DISCLOSURES.md row, appear in no docs/EVIDENCE-LOG.md entry, and are rendered verbatim into the retrievable corpus as citable passages sourced to "data/knowledge/red_flags.yaml" with licence "Ours" (data/knowledge/corpus.yaml:389, :435). Because alcohol is CITED and has a profile_source, it is the one large confounder the dashboard renders as "from a study".
